昼夜節律擾亂行為與妊娠婦女血糖參數及連續血糖監測的關聯

Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism · Associations of Circadian-Disruptive Behaviours With Plasma Glycemic Parameters and Continuous Glucose Monitoring Measures Among Pregnant Women.

研究指出，具備三項或以上昼夜節律擾亂行為（含夜間進食）的孕婦空腹血糖、餐後血糖及葡萄糖AUC顯著升高，且胰島素抵抗增加、β細胞功能下降，提示夜食症可能透過昼夜節律失調惡化孕期代謝健康。

Chronobiology international · Late-night eating, poor sleep quality, and emotional eating: Stronger predictors of overweight and obesity than social jetlag and eating jetlag.
